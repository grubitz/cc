# cc — Catholic calendars

Subscribable `.ics` feeds for holydays of obligation and traditional feasts,
generated by [`ember.py`](ember.py).

**https://grubitz.github.io/cc/**

| Feed | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| `engwal-en.ics` `engwal-pl.ics` | England & Wales |
| `sco-en.ics` `sco-pl.ics` | Scotland |
| `pol-en.ics` `pol-pl.ics` | Poland |
| `ember-en.ics` `ember-pl.ics` | Ember days only |

First part is the locale — whose bishops' conference sets the obligation.
Second is the language of the event titles.

## Two tiers

Every holyday feed carries both, tagged with `CATEGORIES`:

- **Obligation** — what actually binds in that locale today. Says so in the
  title, and names the Sunday when the obligation has been transferred off the
  feast's own date.
- **Traditional** — the wider traditional list plus national patrons. No
  obligation.

A feast in both tiers is emitted once, as Obligation.

## Locale differences

These are not cosmetic, which is why the locales are separate files:

- England & Wales restored Epiphany and Ascension to their proper days in 2017.
  Scotland did not restore Epiphany, so it has five obligations to E&W's six.
- Both transfer Ss Peter & Paul and the Assumption to the adjacent Sunday when
  they fall on a Saturday or Monday. **All Saints differs**: E&W move it either
  way, Scotland only off a Saturday — on a Monday it stays put and binds. So
  1 November 2027 is Sunday 31 October in `engwal-*` and Monday 1 November in
  `sco-*`.
- Poland has no Saturday/Monday transfer at all, and dropped the obligation on
  St Joseph and Ss Peter & Paul in 2003.

## September ember days

The common shorthand — "the Wednesday, Friday and Saturday after Holy Cross,
14 September" — encodes the pre-1960 reckoning, where the First Sunday of
September was the Sunday *nearest* 1 September and so could fall in August.
Under the 1960 rubrics it is the first Sunday *falling in* September, and the
ember week is the one after the Third Sunday. The two agree most years and
diverge by a week in the rest: 2026 is 23, 25 and 26 September, not 16, 18
and 19.

## Building

```
python3 ember.py
```

No dependencies. Writes the `.ics` files into the working directory, and runs
its own assertions first — pinned against published ordos and diocesan lists —
so a wrong calculation fails the build instead of shipping.

The window is a rolling ten years from the current year, so the feeds need
periodic rebuilding to keep gaining years. `.github/workflows/build.yml` does
that monthly and deploys to Pages. **GitHub disables scheduled workflows after
60 days without repository activity** — if the feeds stop advancing, re-enable
it from the Actions tab.

## Adding a locale

Add an entry to `LOCALES` in `ember.py`: its `region` names, `patrons`,
`obligation` list, and a `transfers` map of feast → the weekdays (Mon=0, Sat=5)
on which that obligation moves to the adjacent Sunday. Feasts themselves live
in `HOLYDAYS`, names in `HOLYDAY_NAMES`. Nothing else needs touching.

`UID_DOMAIN` is baked into every event's UID and is how subscribers match
events across refreshes. Changing it after publication orphans every event in
every subscriber's calendar.
